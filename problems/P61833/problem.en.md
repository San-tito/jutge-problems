# Powers of a matrix

Given a $2 \times 2$ matrix $M$ of natural numbers, a natural number $n$
and a natural number $m$, compute $M^n$. To avoid overflows, compute
every element of $M^n \bmod m$.

## Input

Input consists of several cases, each with $M_{11}$, $M_{12}$, $M_{21}$
and $M_{22}$ in this order, followed by $n$ and $m$. Assume that the
elements of $M$ are not larger than $500$, $0 \le n \le 10^9$, and
$2 \le m \le 1000$.

## Output

For every case, print the elements of $M^n \bmod m$ in two lines
following the format of the sample. Print a line with 10 dashes after
every matrix.
