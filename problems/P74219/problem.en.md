# Fibonacci numbers (2)

For every given pair of natural numbers $n$ and $m$, compute
$F_n \bmod m$, where $F_n$ is the $n$-th Fibonacci number (starting at
0).

## Input

The input consists of several pairs of $n$ and $m$. Assume
$0 \le n \le 10^9$ and $2 \le m \le 10^3$.

## Output

For every given pair, print $F_n \bmod m$.
